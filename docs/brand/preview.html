04 · Terminal

Use the wordmark, not a fake icon.

The TUI does not force the message bubble into noisy character art or pretend a lone b is the logo. It uses the plain wordmark and keeps state beside it.

05 · Principles

A quiet Linux identity.

Open-source character comes from clarity, ownership, and restraint—not mascots or platform mimicry.

1

Clear hierarchy

The letter is the bubble. Its tail is part of the same silhouette, not a second icon or badge.

2

One gesture

The tail trails out once, then motion gets out of the conversation's way.

3

Honest fallback

The terminal uses the wordmark instead of reducing the logo to a meaningless letter glyph.
